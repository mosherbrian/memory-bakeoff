if it can show, descriptively and with receipts, that the live memory system
closes the supersession loop on real work while keeping stale actions and human

- S1: at least one real-work supersession cycle closes, with delivered text

- S2: stale-action events are zero, with artifacts checked against the current

- S3: burden is at most one T1 human confirm per working day under tiering, with
  T0 misclassification and unclear counts reported.

  false-fire count and application rate reported.
- S5: overhead stays within +25 percent on paired turns, tokens primary.
- S6: capture-at-rest integrity has zero unsanctioned demotions/losses by scan
