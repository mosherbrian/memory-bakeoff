# Sprint-2 demo — measured results

**Date:** 2026-09-14 sprint close · **Cost:** $0 of a $5 envelope · **Rule:** no outside score imported into our results; vendor numbers are labeled, never claimed.

## Measured results — each with its number

**Memory-trigger smoke test: 36/36 steps match pre-registration.**
A 12-scenario synthetic set exercised the memory trigger end to end after the final corpus fix: every step fired exactly as predicted, including the previously unfireable case. Safety controls held — zero contamination of the live system, zero leakage into real data. *Meaning:* the trigger wiring is proven before any full-scale run; the full run inherits a validated harness, not a hope.

**First privacy-screened outcome bundle: 10 events, automated screen passes with zero findings.**
Ten real operator-correction events crossed into outcome measurement carrying only hashes, counts, and categories — verified to contain no raw transcript text. An independent verifier re-ran the checks and passed them. *Meaning:* real-world calibration data now flows without exposing private content; outcome measurement has its first ground truth.

**Corpus self-tests: 7/7 green, plus a tamper check that bites.**
The test corpus validates itself on every run, and three deliberate corruptions were each caught with exact attribution. *Meaning:* future edits can't silently break the test — the test checks the checker.

**Outside precision benchmark: 13/13 provider rows independently re-derived.**
Every row of an outside benchmark's published table was recomputed from its shipped data and matched, confirming both the table and our finding that its headline column uses a broader denominator than its documentation states. *Meaning:* when we cite outside work, we cite it correctly — and we caught a real documentation error in the process.

**Eight benchmark survey cards closed, licenses verified.**
Eight outside memory benchmarks surveyed for what they measure, goal fit, and reuse rights — four fully reusable, the rest restricted or reference-only. *Meaning:* the next sprint builds adapters only where legally green, with no wasted effort on locked artifacts.

**Integrity suite: 20 automated guards, full sweep green.**
Every guard that protects the evidence pipeline — link validity, number provenance, drift detection — passes; the one known finding is documented and owned. *Meaning:* the numbers above are tamper-checked, not just computed.
