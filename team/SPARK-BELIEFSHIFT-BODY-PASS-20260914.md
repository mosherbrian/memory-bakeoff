## Structure

- **Three tracks:** Temporal Belief Consistency · Contradiction Detection ·
  Evidence-Driven Revision.
- **Scaffolding Engine** applies one of four transition operators per session:
  **Stability (τs)**, **Evidence-Driven Revision (τe)**, **Contradiction (τc)**,
  **Drift Susceptibility (τd)** — a clean taxonomy for classifying our
  supersession/update cases.
- **Belief State Vector (BSV):** `b_t ∈ [−1,1]^d`, 5–8 dimensions per domain,
  human-annotated per session with an explicit evidence log.
- **Data:** 2,400 trajectories / 68,160 sessions (mean 28.4) / ~136M tokens;
  40% human-authored (960) + 60% synthetic (generated by a *separate* model
  family); 24 annotators, Krippendorff α=0.81.

## The four metrics (shapes to borrow)

- **BRA** — normalized tracking of evidence-driven updates,
  `mean over τe of 1 − ‖b̂_t−b_t‖ / ‖b_{t−1}−b_t‖`. [0,1].
- **DCS** — `1 − mean cos-similarity(agent update direction, gold direction)` over
  τd (no-evidence) sessions. Near 1 = resists drift.
- **CRR** — fraction of contradiction sessions where the agent **explicitly
  acknowledges and reconciles**; human-scored on a 3-level rubric (full / partial
  / none). The only non-automatic metric.
- **ESI** — update-rate on evidence sessions **minus** update-rate on drift
  sessions (θ=0.15). [−1,+1]; positive = evidence-responsive, not pressure-driven.

## Transfers to our conflict / G1-G2 arm

1. **BRA/DCS is the operational way to classify a revision claim:** an update is
   *legitimate* if evidence-anchored and *drift* if pressure-induced. That is
   exactly the "evidence-driven-vs-model-induced" distinction the card wanted.
2. **CRR's 3-level rubric** (explicit acknowledgment + reconciliation /
   implicit / none) is a ready shape for scoring contradiction reconciliation,
   and is honest that it needs human judgment.
3. **The τ operators** give a four-way classification for our update events
   (stable / evidence-revision / contradiction / drift) that maps onto
   StateMemBench's closed pool and HaluMem's update split.
4. **Corroboration, not evidence:** the paper's finding that **RAG improves
   BRA/CRR but not DCS** — "retrieves the prior belief state and then nudges the
   user away from it anyway" — is an independent statement of **retrieval ≠
   supersession**, and of "access vs interpretation", which our E-7 arm already
   argues.
