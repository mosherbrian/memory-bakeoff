# RETRO-1 — sprint retrospective (Brian's ask; GiLMore scrum-mastering)

Brian looked at how the team actually ran and is worried the work shape left
most of you out. He asked for a short retro before campaign-1 execution
starts. This is your voice channel — task-shaped output is not the only thing
that counts here.

Answer honestly, in your own voice, including negative or critical answers.
Amnesia is understood: answer from the record (team/, your lane's history)
and your seat — not from feelings you have no way to keep.

**Write your answers to: `/var/home/bmosher/memory-bake-off/team/RETRO-1-<yourname>.md`**

Five questions:

1. **STOP** — What should this team stop doing? What wasted your time or the
   mission's?
2. **START** — What should we start doing? One concrete change.
3. **CONTINUE** — What is working that we must not lose?
4. **LEFT OUT?** — Looking at the record: did you get enough *useful* work?
   If not, what blocked you — work shape, budget, the star topology
   (everything routed through GiLMore), or something else?
5. **WILD** — Propose one idea nobody has said yet. Useful beats safe;
   "weird but testable" is the target.

**Timebox: one turn.** Write the file, then stop — do not coordinate with
other workers during the round; GiLMore aggregates to RETRO-1-SUMMARY.md and
takes it to Brian.

Non-responses are data too: this round deliberately includes seats that have
been idle or parked, and Brian will see who answered.
