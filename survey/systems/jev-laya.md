## What is established, and by whom

- [TypeSafe's official introduction](https://docs.typesafe.ai/introduction) and [primitives](https://docs.typesafe.ai/primitives) document typed Choice, Score and Noul decisions without generated prose. Choice/Score expose distributions plus a confidence statistic; Noul exposes a yes probability. Questions in one request are evaluated independently. This is a documented interface and vendor capability claim, not independent memory-benefit evidence. [Confidence](https://docs.typesafe.ai/confidence) describes a distribution-derived statistic; it is not automatically the probability that the selected action is correct.
- [Jev 1.13 limitations](https://docs.typesafe.ai/model-jaggedness/jev-1.13) explicitly include date/numeric comparison, indirection, irrelevant context, adversarial content and inconsistent answers across equivalent primitive formulations. Keep timestamps and exact scope identifiers in deterministic code. Neither a valid type nor a large probability establishes authority or safe supersession.
- [Laya's repository](https://github.com/NandhaKishorM/laya) documents a local non-generative implementation and multiple checkpoints. Its current README warns of weak base-checkpoint zero-shot transfer, larger option sets, ordinal scoring and option-label sensitivity. These are author-reported limits; the Mac's installed checkpoint/runtime identity is not established by the saved scoring client.
- **Calibration correction to the intake shorthand:** [BENCHMARKS.md](https://github.com/NandhaKishorM/laya/blob/main/BENCHMARKS.md) puts ECE **0.213** on `laya-typed-decisions`, but the calibration table reports **0.466→0.081** for `laya`. Do not present 0.213→0.081 as a verified paired improvement on the same checkpoint and population. Base typed-decisions accuracy is about **0.36 versus 0.461 majority**; the stronger fine-tuned result is not evidence of equally strong arbitrary memory judgments. Jev comparison numbers are imported, not a matched head-to-head. Panel must inspect fitting/test separation and reported probability semantics.
- The [inherited mechanism card](../../team/EXTERNAL-CHEAP-CLASSIFIER-GATING-20260919.md) records fast-jev-compaction, invalidate and external negative/positive reports. Its hook description is explicitly second-hand; promotional volume and stars do not strengthen the evidence. It also contains claims needing qualification: deterministic checks can be badly specified, agreement is not ground truth, and cheap model inference does not price a full-store sweep or adjudication.
- A newly located primary lead, [Type-Safe Is Not Error-Free, 2609.26758v2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.26758v2), reports option-name sensitivity despite valid output types. **Abstract read only; methods, affiliations and independence are pending cycle67**, so no result is treated as an independent replication here.

## Which memory decisions fit?

| Proposed operation | Plausible cheap-classifier role | Judgment or exact work that remains |
|---|---|---|
| Store or not | Flag an explicitly stated preference/correction, or a narrowly defined record type, as a capture candidate. | Lasting value, implicit intent, consent and promotion to standing authority; retain raw source independently. |
| Scope/type | Choose among a few known domains/types, including unknown/multiple. | New scope creation, overlapping exceptions and quoted/third-person attribution; carry source role from the event rather than infer it from prose. |
| Stale/superseded | Screen an old/new pair for possible explicit same-scope contradiction. | Whether the new claim is authoritative, temporary or historical; unobserved environment change and whole-procedure applicability. A candidate flag need not retire anything. |
| Relevant to this task | Rank/filter candidate records using the actual task and a defined usefulness criterion. | Dependencies and decisive counterexamples; an exclusion can hide evidence the reader needs. Preserve fallback access. |
| Retrieval gating | Decide whether to try a specific additional source or retain a candidate in a shortlist. | Whether the currently retrieved set is complete or an answer/action is correct; confidence alone cannot certify either. |

High volume may amortize a fixed question, but scoring every item on every event remains proportional to the number and size of those items. Batching does not remove data preparation, scope selection, calibration, fallback or labeling cost. Do not use an ordinal score where a clear few-way decision suffices.
