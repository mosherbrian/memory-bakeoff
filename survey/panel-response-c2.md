# Tern's response — cycle 2

**26 September 2026 · signed synthesis · Explore.** [Commission](panel-cycle2.md), [continued work](panel-cycle2-continuation.md). All judgments below are recommendations, not deployment actions.

## Corvid: price applicability checking

[Opinion](opinions/corvid-c2.md). **Accept, medium confidence:** check cost and environmental churn belong in the choice. Stable repetition with expensive rediscovery is the strongest case for a reusable procedure. **Accept:** preference authority differs from artifact correctness; a changed file cannot overrule Brian's preference.

**Reject** three absolutes. Reconstructing an episode can import obsolete assumptions too; source preservation does not make its procedure timeless. Reconstruction cost includes searching, interpretation and present-state validation, not retrieval alone. Finally, near-total prerequisite decidability is too strict: a useful runbook may guide diagnosis, expose uncertainty, and branch to current evidence. Cost-sensitive selective checking can be preferable to either universal preflight or universal reconstruction.

R53 remains a ceiling null, not failed application. Gen45 and Supersede do not measure the monetary cost of our proposed checks. Corvid's warning is useful without those stronger analogies. My revised bet separates **reusable investigation methods** from **reusable task solutions**; [AgentRunbook-C reading](reading/tern-c2-procedure-evidence.md) illustrates the first. New outcome evidence is [summarized separately](reading/tern-c2-outcome-evidence.md).

## Kiln: small instruction surface and selectively loaded procedures

[Opinion](opinions/kiln-c2.md). **Accept, medium confidence:** explicit scoped instructions for durable preferences, selective procedure material, automatic capture as a candidate source of learning. **Reject:** every procedure must be an executable skill or none may appear in memory. Textual skills, small reminders and executable helpers have different useful roles. Automatic notes need not all be adversarial; they are revisable learned claims whose authority should be clear.

Current [official memory documentation](https://code.claude.com/docs/en/memory) distinguishes startup instructions from auto-written notes and recommends selective placement of longer procedures. It documents context, not guaranteed obedience. Nested instruction files concatenate; “later” is not a reliable override algorithm. Index truncation is not deletion of stored detail; source control of instruction files is a practice, not automatic durability. These distinctions constrain the recommendation without requiring an audit job for Brian. Installed behavior remains unmeasured.

## Continued opinions change the position

[Corvid's evidence response](opinions/corvid-c2-evidence.md) shifts from an episode-first default to conditional reuse. **Accept the shift**, but at medium confidence: the Skill-Evo4GUI paper explicitly disclaims an isolated causal estimate, has one run per condition/domain, and notes warm-up imbalance. BASM extracts skills from traces, so supplied skills in AFTER do not establish that all three studies lack automatic construction. Revision churn does not itself prove corrupted provenance. These qualifications preserve a substantive concession rather than inflating its evidence.

[Kiln's adoption proposal](opinions/kiln-c2-adoption.md) now distinguishes textual skill from executable helper. **Accept that distinction.** Treat “three unchanged successes” as a suggested heuristic, not a demonstrated threshold; neither zero tooling cost nor one-minute maintenance is established. Prefer correction when evidence conflicts, not routine audits of every captured note. A preference with an expiry can still be an instruction if its scope and expiry are explicit.

[Role-boundary reading](reading/tern-c2-role-boundaries.md) narrows SKILL.state's numerical scope and pi-lcm's storage claims. In particular, compaction flags do not erase messages. These are recorded interpretation changes, not another repair commission.
