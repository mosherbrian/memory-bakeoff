# Cycle47 decision — inference belongs in memory, without becoming authority

Tern ·26 September2026 · all three panel pieces read · Explore. [Primary-method note](reading/tern-c47-writer-reader-controls.md).

**Memo change:** useful inferred tendencies may be agent-written into the record, with their interpretation and attribution kept revisable. Do not turn hypothetical or third-person material into Brian's profile just because it appeared in his conversation. This sharpens the existing inference/authority split, not a new approval or per-record checklist.

**What the study changes:** PersonaMem-v2 adds open-ended evaluation and jointly trained memory writing/answering. The result supports a combined learning approach, not a portable record-format gain with the reader held fixed. Compact answer context is valuable, while full construction, updates, training and user effort still matter. Native-first remains a practical starting point, not a claim that reader capability is irrelevant.

**Corvid:** accept inference as a useful mechanism, reject “a record cannot hold a preference never stated”: agents can record inferences, as the memo already allows. This is not just a storage/authority design. The current opinion is explicitly abstract-level; its response-selection-only characterization misses §4.1's open-ended judge protocol. Similar headline percentages cannot partition writer versus reader contributions. We do not release a test or training run from this recommendation. [Opinion](opinions/corvid-c47.md).

**Kiln:** accept sequential capped-memory construction and the difference between framework and host facility. No source/history mechanism described is not proof that source retention is impossible outside it, or that all provenance ends at text. Reject “most deployment-shaped” without comparing actual integrations. Training cost is not necessarily paid by a vendor, trained weights are not categorically outside Brian's interests, and a cap alone does not establish cheap reuse. It bounds the final view, not the complete work. No universal receipt requirement or capped-rewrite adoption follows. [Card](systems/personamem-v2.md), [opinion](opinions/kiln-c47.md).

**Cairn:** accept synthetic construction, open-ended scoring and missing fixed-reader isolation. The coupled system comparison is still memory-system evidence; “not a memory effect” is too categorical. Named train/validation/test counts need the useful added boundary: training personas do not overlap benchmark personas. Hardware/steps are reported even though a full cost ledger is not. Forget requests in a benchmark do not establish physical deletion or measured human correction burden. “Brian cannot train” is not a supplied constraint, nor is headline performance automatically transferable to his lane. Most preferences are implicit, not necessarily every preference; scenario classes are new relative to this pair, not claimed field firsts. [Read](reading/c47-personamem-v2.md).

**Position:** judge the combined agent-plus-memory arrangement on useful behavior; isolate components when deciding which component to change. Retain source-grounded inferences as inferences and explicit directions as directions. Medium confidence; no deployment winner.
