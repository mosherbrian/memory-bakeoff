# Practical arrangements: choose the work removed

**Tern · cycle31 ·26 September2026 · advice, not deployment.**

**Start with existing host facilities and agent-owned upkeep, medium confidence.** Keep a small reusable procedure core, explicit directions and linked evidence. Assign capture, revision and host refresh to agents/operators already doing the work; do not silently assign them to Brian. The complete cross-host arrangement remains unmeasured. This recommendation uses the current stack while keeping automation candidates genuinely competitive.

**Letta is the runtime alternative**, rather than an interchangeable add-on. Its memory editing/versioning and integrated delivery warrant comparison when changing the runtime is acceptable. Learned policies or adapted weights are another axis: they can coexist with these arrangements, with separate acquisition/correction costs. Neither is the default answer to a missing host refresh.

**Decision rule:** identify the work currently unsupplied—capture, correction, discovery, host loading or application—and choose the lightest arrangement that supplies it reliably. Do not require an arbitrary number of repeated incidents. Both agent-maintained files and services consume agent compute, operating effort and judgment; neither reduces cleanly to minutes versus dollars. No current evidence establishes cross-host delivery as Brian's measured dominant bottleneck.

**Across all arrangements:** share durable learning by scope; hand off live task context explicitly. Keep source history, uncertain hypotheses, current guidance and sponsor directions distinguishable. Preserve relevant disagreements before resolving them. Neither a passing narrow check nor a high confidence score certifies an entire procedure. No mandatory per-record counter or five-use retirement rule follows from benchmark ablations.

**What would change the choice:** a concrete candidate delivering useful corrections with less combined rediscovery, misapplication and upkeep on the hosts Brian uses. We have enough evidence to give starting advice; we do not yet have a comparative user-cost winner. [Earlier arrangement history](snapshots/ALTERNATIVES-through-c30.md).
