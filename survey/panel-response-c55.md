# Cycle55 — useful correction without a new maintenance ritual

Tern ·26 September2026 · all three pieces read; existing evidence only.

**Decision:** retain agent-owned upkeep, editable scoped guidance and recoverable evidence. A correction is useful when it affects the later relevant response or action. This is a success criterion, not a compulsory agent audit after every correction. The three user studies do not establish a lower-cost alternative arrangement. [Lead evidence comparison](reading/tern-c55-user-evidence-synthesis.md).

**Corvid:** accept removing unnecessary narration and review for already-authorized routine changes. Reject a universal consequential-change approval gate and a universal log duty. Consequence, ambiguity and authorization guide review; neither “mechanical” nor “low stakes” removes the need to understand the correction. Production formation, staged editing and observational field use support different claims, not a tested two-track policy. [Opinion](opinions/corvid-c55.md).

**Kiln:** the proposed path is a useful decomposition, not five mandatory steps. A direct scoped correction need not wait for closeout, become a skill, acquire a disconfirmer or trigger a reload that the host does not need. Do not prescribe Brian reviewing every commit. Source-history storage and a filtered derived record are different; pi-lcm does not by itself implement capture into a canonical instruction file. Existing facilities plus conventions remain a candidate arrangement, not a measured persistence win. [Opinion](opinions/kiln-c55.md).

**Cairn:** accept the separation of formation, staged correction and repeated-use reports. Two errors recur: c52's command-regex result is not a capture-labor measurement; Semantic Commit reports18 cases involving10 participants, not10 of18 cases. The elapsed-time difference is observed, not a universal price per three changes. Silence is not consent, but neither is it diagnostic evidence that correction failed. A later relevant failure is a sensible occasion to inspect the path; no universal post-correction audit follows from these sources. [Reading](reading/c55-correction-evidence.md).

**What changes in the memo:** no new mechanism. Keep “appropriate later use” explicit and avoid turning evidence gaps into duties. When a correction demonstrably recurs, trace the present failure: stale source, missed update, delivery, scope interpretation or action. The choice of remedy remains contextual. Medium confidence in advice; comparative savings for Brian remain unmeasured.

**Next:** return to Brian's first pain, procedural re-learning. Cycle56 uses already-read procedure studies and system cards to produce one worked, minimal arrangement and identify what the agent can stop doing. No new source sweep, experiment, install or intake execution.
