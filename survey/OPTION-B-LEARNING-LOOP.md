# Option B — learning from corrections without certifying its own errors

Tern · cycle81 sponsor extension ·26 September2026. Proposed addition to the [cascade](OPTION-B-CASCADE.md); no labeling, training, sampling or model run. **Verdict: include the loop, with confidence medium in its structure and low in demonstrated local benefit.** It gives the screener a way to encounter misses and gives the judge/reflector reusable experience. It does not turn their decisions into independent truth.

## One design, two learning mechanisms

Claude remains proposed owner of the cascade, model releases and example corpus. Stage2 verdicts and nightly outcomes become **provisional labeled examples**; Brian's explicit corrections/reverts have priority where their meaning and scope are clear. Keep source/revision, label origin, relation/scope, superseded label and model/prompt versions. A consolidated decision and the judge verdict it accepted are one correlated episode, not two independent votes. A revert may indicate a new preference, changed scope or bad timing rather than prove the old relation was always false. Conflicting human/LLM labels are resolved in favor of the applicable explicit direction, not merely averaged with a larger weight; factual claims still depend on evidence.

**Weight learning:** periodically fine-tune Laya on eligible training examples, including sampled misses, and fit temperatures on separate calibration data. Do not retrain merely because one more night elapsed if useful new labels are absent. Keep prior model, tokenizer, calibration temperatures, prompt/schema and threshold together as a rollbackable release. A model rollback does not undo already published replacement links; those have separate revision/rollback handling.

**Prompt learning:** keep the judge and reflector weights fixed. Retrieve a small relevant set of worked cases from the same eligible corpus: source pair/context, scoped decision, rationale and observed correction/outcome. Include counterexamples and overturned judgments; exclude superseded labels and every frozen-test case from retrieval as well as training. Examples guide judgment, never outrank Brian's explicit direction. Keep this inside the existing cascade's versioned corpus, not another service.

## Required guards and their precise meaning

1. **Nightly exploration outside the filter.** Randomly sample unflagged pairs from the defined candidate universe, record its size and sampling probability, and send them to the judge without exposing the screener's score. Keep daytime non-suppression. This counters selection bias among generated pairs; it cannot discover pairs that candidate generation omitted. Define the binary screening target and mapping from the four relation labels first; `unresolved` must not silently become a negative.
2. **Report a weighted, judge-referenced live recall estimate.** If all flagged pairs are judged, let TP be judged-positive flagged pairs, U the total unflagged count, n the random unflagged sample and m its judged positives. Estimated misses = U×m/n; estimated recall = TP/(TP + U×m/n). With unequal/stratified sampling use the recorded inclusion probabilities. Report counts and uncertainty; zero observed misses in a small sample does not establish 0.98 recall. Independent hand labels are needed to check judge error. Keep this live estimate distinct from recall on the frozen gold test, and from end-to-end coverage. This is a design estimator, not a computed result.
3. **Frozen hand-labeled test, separate training and calibration.** Preserve Brian's requested promotion gate through `laya.evals`: recall and calibration must not regress, and recall must also meet the stated target—not merely match a poor incumbent. Specify the metric, tolerance/uncertainty and relevant slices before evaluation. Calibration is separate from threshold recall; aggregate ECE alone can hide rare-positive errors, so retain a proper probability score and class/score-band checks. Compare workload/pass-through too: always-flag achieves full screening recall without saving judge work. Cycle82 source inspection confirms supplied report/baseline/threshold exit-code gates, but not built-in binary recall/precision, automatic promotion/rollback or temperature fitting in the evaluation path. Cycle83 identifies `noul` as P(yes), distinct from selected-class `answer_confidence`. Map yes to flag explicitly; custom recall must use the serving probability threshold, not the stock0.5 correctness calculation. Custom metrics can reach CLI gates, but computing recall remains evaluator-extension work. Retain the old release on failed or inconclusive promotion. Never tune on the frozen test or retrieve it into prompts; repeated promotion decisions can still overfit a fixed benchmark, so it is not a permanent guarantee of live performance.
4. **Human evidence outranks pseudo-label consensus.** Prioritize Brian's applicable corrections over model labels; preserve uncertain and disputed labels rather than manufacturing gold. “The student cannot exceed a wrong teacher” is not a universal ceiling—other evidence and generalization can improve it—but repeating the teacher's unchecked errors gives this loop no reason to correct them. Accepted nightly output is a training candidate, not proof that the replacement worked later.

## Cycle82 dissent — fixed screening before recurring retraining

Corvid prefers fixed local screening plus retrieved worked examples for the judge, avoiding recurring model releases. Tern retains it as the simpler comparator, not a rejection of Brian's loop. A fixed screen still needs a declared rule/model and operating point; worked-example upkeep remains. Random unflagged sampling covers only generated pairs. A judge/reference disagreement alone does not justify training the screener: locate screening misses or excessive pass-through and show that a trained candidate improves that tradeoff on independent unseen examples with the judge held fixed. Preserve four relation labels plus unresolved status. [Panel response](panel-response-c82.md). No new test commissioned.


**Cycle82 feasibility update:** [Laya evaluation primitives](reading/c82-laya-promotion-interface.md) supply an extension point and build-gate exit code. Keep custom screening metrics and release handling inside this design; neither is deployed. Calibration fitting is separate from the evaluated scoring path. An empirical threshold remains meaningful without interpreting its score as a calibrated probability. The original46-case probe does not supply the new task's label budget. [Qualifications](panel-response-c82.md).


**Cycle83 simpler branch:** Corvid proposes omitting the trained screener and using direct provisional judging plus a final-view publication gate. Accept as a sequencing rival. Random-unflagged sampling can be omitted only when all generated pairs are judged; any retained exclusion screen still needs the guard for live recall claims. Judge-error checks and candidate coverage remain. Both proposed integrations are unbuilt, despite belonging inside existing component boundaries. [Disposition](panel-response-c83.md).


**Cycle83 probability/gate contract:** use `noul` P(yes) for yes=flag, not selected-class `answer_confidence`. The decision threshold producing flag/keep and the CLI metric threshold accepting a release are different parameters. Keep evaluation and serving checkpoint/temperature/threshold aligned. The supplied CLI can gate custom metrics, but a binary recall evaluator is still needed; no new scorer framework is proposed. Source semantics do not establish empirical calibration or installed-instance equivalence. [Pinned card](systems/laya-binary-eval-contract.md), [lead scope](panel-response-c83.md).


**Cycle83 label contract:** the existing benchmark notes do not establish a ready-made four-way or binary screening corpus; this is not proof of absence from the underlying datasets. Screening gold means “needs relationship adjudication before deciding whether guidance still applies,” not “same topic” or “will be replaced.” Consider overlapping scope/time and dependent attributes; unresolved applicability routes to review. Coexistence can be a reviewed outcome without making every compatible pair a positive. Independent annotations, matched non-update negatives and explicit unresolved handling are needed; no labels were created. [Label review and qualifications](panel-response-c83.md).
