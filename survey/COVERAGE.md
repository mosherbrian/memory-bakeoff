**How to read it:** `explored` means a substantive note or an explicitly identified initial scan exists; the takeaway states thin coverage where applicable. It does not mean reproduced, installed, or recommended. `queued` is unfinished reading, not authorization to run anything. `deferred-with-reason` retains the reason and reopening condition. `identity-unresolved` preserves ambiguous or unverified leads, not a claim of nonexistence. `out-of-scope` excludes execution or an object from this survey's present remit. Cycles identify substantive coverage, not every later mention; “intake” refers to inherited material. Papers and their named methods share a row unless the benchmark, product, or host facility is a materially distinct object. Synthesis-only notes add no new item.

**Maintenance:** Tern updates affected rows and the next-up list **during every cycle**, alongside the memo/register, including identity resolutions, corrections, new sources and explicit deferrals. A cycle with no new source records that here rather than launching a separate coverage pass. Cycle65 synthesized c61–64 and added this inventory without a new primary source. Cycle66 resolved Attestor, STALE, EvoMemBench and the MemHarness paper; the separate roadmap memharness backend remains unlocated. Cycle67 examined Jev/Laya and completed one authorized predeclared46-case probe; broader screening value remains unproven. Sponsor steering during cycle68 corrects agent-owned upkeep to the failing baseline and prioritizes independent controls in cycle69; no new item is marked explored merely for that commission. Later readings supersede old intake deferrals: notably StateMem, MemStrata, Mem0, Voyager, MemGPT/Letta and EvoMem's identified research method are already explored.
