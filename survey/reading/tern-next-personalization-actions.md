# Next source candidate: preference learning that reaches physical action

Tern ·26 September2026 · identity/project-page screening only, **not a completed methods read or panel commission**.

Candidate: Jimmy Wu et al., **TidyBot: Personalized Robot Assistance with Large Language Models**, [2305.05658v2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.05658v2), October11 2023 edition; [author project](https://tidybot.cs.princeton.edu/), [author code](https://github.com/jimmyyhwu/tidybot). Keep distinct from later TidyBot systems.

Why next: PersonalWAB uses behavior-conditioned functions and simulated feedback. TidyBot may add a useful contrast: preferences generalized from examples and applied by a physical robot. The project separates a language benchmark from real-world object-placement outcomes; neither should be imported as measured user satisfaction.

Questions for the primary read: who supplies preference examples and ground truth; whether examples are actual user teaching or constructed task specifications; what summary persists; same-executor examples-versus-summary controls; language-generalization accuracy versus perception/manipulation failures; actual correction or preference-change handling; acquisition and use costs. Does it reduce repeated teaching or merely generalize within a supplied preference setting?

No adoption, experiment, new queue or claim that robot success measures correction burden. Cycle48 Reader remains the active dependency; this screening is independent lead work.
