**My judgment.** Try a small inferred rule when examples reveal a reusable distinction and the executor can apply it. This is an alternative worth considering before training, not cross-paper proof that training loses. Induction is agent authorship; it is already compatible with our memo. The summary's usefulness includes selecting distinctions the downstream system needs—not merely shortening text.

Keep three limits explicit: a language placement score is not physical success; physical success is not reduced teaching burden; a perception result does not establish software-host transfer. Lack of good examples does not logically require training—direct instructions, clarification, observation or a simpler default may suffice. Re-teaching to regenerate a summary is plausible design, not a demonstrated lifecycle on the inspected evidence.
