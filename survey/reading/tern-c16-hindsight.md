# Hindsight: distinguish the paper, current product and tested configuration

**Tern · 26 September 2026 · primary reading, no installation or experiment.**

**Paper mechanism.** [Hindsight v1, §§3–5 and 7](https://arxiv.org/html/2512.12818v1) separates extracted world/experience facts, synthesized observations and confidence-bearing opinions. Retention already involves narrative extraction, so “fact” is a representation label rather than guaranteed ground truth. Retrieval combines several routes before reranking; reflection adds a behavioral profile and belief updates. The evaluation supports conversational QA, not a measured reduction in procedure rediscovery or repeated preference corrections. It also does not isolate each architectural component's contribution. A confidence-bearing agent opinion is not equivalent to a sponsor's explicit direction. **Medium confidence in relevance, limited task transfer.**

**Current product evidence.** The [observation documentation](https://hindsight.vectorize.io/developer/observations) describes links and quotes to supporting memories, revisions with retained history, scoped consolidation and checks against newer facts when observations are stale. This is more than naming categories; it weakens a blanket “labels without provenance” objection. However, quoted supporting memories need not be verbatim original transcripts if an earlier extraction transformed them. A support count is not a count of independent witnesses or a truth guarantee. The documented source-deletion behavior also matters to what remains recoverable. Vendor descriptions of preserved history and correct reconciliation remain implementation claims, not our measured outcome. **Medium confidence in documented functionality; source-to-original fidelity unverified.**

**Integration correction.** The [coding-agents changelog, 0.5.0](https://hindsight.vectorize.io/changelog/integrations/coding-agents) explicitly adds Pi support. The card's claim of no listed Pi integration is outdated; actual installed interoperability remains untested. The [model documentation](https://hindsight.vectorize.io/developer/models) describes `claude-code` as a subscription-backed provider. A local CLI/daemon does not make its model inference local, and it does not establish zero cost. These distinctions change the integration assessment, not an installation decision.

**Judgment:** Hindsight is a stronger practical integrated comparator than an abstract-only entry suggested. Preserve the difference between a raw/no-LLM historical test and the full pipeline; neither proves the other's behavior. Still watch, not deploy: the relevant unresolved question is whether its extra interpretation and delivery steps save Brian effort while preserving the evidence needed to correct mistakes. No arbitrary repeat-count threshold or new mandatory lifecycle trial follows.

**Original-source follow-up (10:03Z):** current [retain documentation](https://hindsight.vectorize.io/developer/retain) explicitly says documents remain stored even when extraction yields zero facts. Such documents are not discoverable through recall/reflect; document listing exposes their zero memory count, and reprocessing can recover them without re-upload. Verbatim and chunk modes differ from default extraction. Thus “original text is not retained” is not supported; the narrower open issue is the route from a returned memory to its original and whether the host exposes it. Preservation and discoverability are distinct documented properties, not a new observed defect. Kiln is tracing that route.
