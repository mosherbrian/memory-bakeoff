## When reuse amplifies a bad abstraction → don't store; fix the default

DC's own §5: faulty heuristics in memory are *"equally amplified"* — and its curator is
label-blind `[read]`. The ordinary-tool remedy is not a better memory; it is a **default that
fails loudly**: a checker that re-runs, a template that regenerates, a pinned version. For
Brian's stack, where tools and paths shift, a stored "worked" claim decays into confident
wrongness that reuse *scales*. The cheap tool (re-run the check) beats the expensive memory
(curate the lesson) whenever the check exists.

## When it genuinely saves rediscovery → store, narrowly

DC's dramatic wins (Game of 24 10→99; Equation Balancer →98–100) share one property: **the
family recurs and its rules are stable** `[read]`. Store when recurrence is *observed* (twice
or more) and the environment is stable between occurrences — then carry the check that marked
"worked" with the artifact (c22's affordance, offered not imposed). BASM's angle `[note]`:
selection is applicability + repair, and self-judged lessons institutionalise mistakes —
another reason the *check*, not the *lesson*, is what should travel.

## When predictability permits preparation → schedule, don't remember

Sleep-time Compute `[note]`: preparation on known context amortises when future work is
predictable — with two recorded limits (SWE precision = file-overlap, not executable
correctness; background work has a cost; query-time savings ≠ total savings). The deeper
lesson for Brian's lane: **ordinary tooling removes the arm rather than improving it.** A
timer/cron removes *selection* (the schedule picks the runbook — no retrieval-precision needed);
a template removes *authoring*; grep removes *retrieval*; a checker removes the stale "worked"
claim. Memory is the remedy of **last resort**, for what tooling cannot remove: cross-cutting
preferences and procedures whose shape was not anticipated.

## One recommendation to withhold rather than universalise

My c21 line *"store the ran-and-worked artifact"* — **withhold it as universal.** For one-off
or environment-sensitive procedures, storing is net-negative (amplification risk + upkeep with
no recurrence to amortise over). Condition it on observed recurrence, and let non-recurrence
be the default verdict for anything stored only once.
