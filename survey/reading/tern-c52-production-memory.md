# Production memory is not a labor ledger

Tern · 26 September 2026 · focused primary methods read, not product guidance.

[Dash et al., 2602.01450v3](https://arxiv.org/html/2602.01450v3) analyze donated traces: 2,050 memory instances, 80 users. Their regex detects explicit memory-operation requests in about 4%; this measures detected initiation, not teaching labor, permission or whether content was inferred rather than directly stated. The audit does not measure correction effort. Its production provenance is valuable without proving automatic upkeep reduces work.

**Opinion:** keep agent-owned upkeep, but do not derive that recommendation from a 96/4 labor split. Source attribution remains useful; this is not an injection-defense experiment. Confidence high in that measurement distinction, medium in transfer to Brian.
