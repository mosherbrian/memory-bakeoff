Identity verified: Quan et al., *From Momentary Emotion Inference to Sustained Emotion Support: Evaluating a Companion Agent in a Longitudinal Study*, [arXiv2609.16344v2](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.16344v2). Independent read: §§4.2,5,6.3–6.4,7.2–7.3; technical emotion-estimation baselines kept separate from deployment comparison.

**Question:** do retained corrections change later helpful behavior, and what does the study actually isolate? Inspect exposure and attrition, user-owned corrections, stored representation, retrieval, longitudinal analysis and comparator. Separate overall helpfulness from a causal memory effect and clinical benefit. No inference yet from the abstract to Brian's software workflows.

**Finding:** 24 recruited,19 completed a14-day deployment;1,093 sessions. Fixed daily touchpoints and compensation constrain voluntary-use inference. The single-system deployment measures actual repeated interaction; no memory ablation isolates the memory contribution. Helpfulness rose, but familiarity and changed narration are competing explanations. No clinical or coding-workflow efficacy follows.

**Correction path:** an understanding rating<=4 elicits correction text. After an evening session, a model summarizes the day into a structured block capped at150 words, including corrections and prior outcomes; later appraisal/conversation calls consume it. This documents intended delivery, not successful propagation of each correction. It does not establish a cron trigger, exclusive admission gate, complete version history or tested user-editable memory interface.

**Most relevant evidence:** §6.4.2 reports six participants noticing incorporated feedback and nine describing non-persistent corrections OR eventually stopping correction. These categories need not be disjoint; nine is not a count of observed quitters. Examples include repeated clarification, old events treated as current, and occasional activities interpreted as habitual. These are interview reports about behavior, not a correction-ID-to-later-action audit. They strengthen the practical concern without locating failure at capture, summary, retrieval or interpretation.

The gated correction texts and guidance-adoption responses are separate datasets. Their counts cannot estimate all memory failures or the cost of correcting them. Nor does lower valence improvement in corrected sessions show that correcting caused harm: assignment was not randomized.

**Decision:** a useful correction must change relevant later behavior, rather than only the immediate acknowledgment. Preserve agent-owned upkeep and recoverable sources; when a correction recurs, investigate the path carrying it forward. This adds a better success criterion, not universal tests or a new user rating form. The150-word cap and rating threshold are not demonstrated fixes. Inspectable objects, dates, source/inference distinctions and propagation are proposed design implications in §7.3.3, not evaluated facilities here. Medium confidence in practical transfer; high confidence in the observational/causal distinction. No experiment or install.
