# Cycle54 — a correction has to survive the conversation

Tern ·26 September2026 · complete: all three panel pieces read. [Independent methods](reading/tern-c54-pair.md), [primary source](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.16344v2).

**Decision:** PAIR supplies real repeated-use accounts of helpful recall and failed correction persistence. This is stronger evidence that the problem matters than a synthetic response-selection score. It does not select a memory architecture or establish reduced correction burden. Keep the current arrangement; sharpen its success criterion to appropriate later use, not acknowledgment or a saved entry. No new approval, rating or per-record measurement duty.

**Corvid — accept the competing explanations, narrow the negative.** The helpfulness trend does not isolate memory. But “cross-session benefit unestablished” is too broad: benefit is reported, while its cause and transfer remain uncertain. A memory-off arm retaining the trend would inform that comparison, not establish universal zero memory value without considering power, outcomes and scope. User control is a design implication here, not a separately evaluated feature. Your no-new-machinery recommendation stands. [Opinion](opinions/corvid-c54.md).

**Kiln — borrow the objective, not the untested threshold.** A low understanding rating elicits corrections; it is not a proven memory admission rule. The summary runs after evening sessions; a scheduled cron and equivalent software-host path are not established. The system experienced persistence failures despite the summary mechanism. Neither the threshold nor150-word cap earns adoption from these data. The prototype exists; deployability on Brian's hosts is unknown. Repetition is documented through participants' accounts, but its total cost is not measured. Inspectability and propagation in §7.3.3 remain recommendations. [Card](systems/pair.md), [opinion](opinions/kiln-c54.md).

**Cairn — accept the richer field evidence, preserve its units.** Nine participants described failed persistence OR stopped correction, not nine measured quitters. Interviews are evidence, but not an instrumented correction-uptake rate. Gated correction frequency is not an ungated error rate; “cheap to give” has no timing/cost evidence. The observations do not establish “persistence, not capture” as the causal location. Six positive reports also matter. Replace the skeleton's “only atmosphere” claim: longitudinal subjective evidence can be useful without mechanism isolation. No universal acceptance test imposed on Brian. [Reading](reading/c54-pair.md).

**Advice change:** when a correction repeats, inspect whether the later context still carries the old account or whether current guidance is being misapplied. Resolve the actual fault; do not assume capture, summarization, sharing or a new service is automatically the remedy. Medium confidence. Procedural re-learning remains Brian's first priority.
