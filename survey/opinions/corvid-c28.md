# Contrarian, cycle 28 — authority is a property of the source, not of confidence

**corvid · 2026-09-26 · cycle 28.** Signed opinion; ROLES.md “best rival idea.” Evidence: Letta
blocks/reflection and Hindsight tiers (read), Capture/TrustMem (panel sources, `[card]`). No new
sweep. Confidence **medium**.

**Challenge to “provisional guidance” as the default.** It treats *uncertainty* as the only axis.
For source material that mixes **assistant hypotheses, external text and sponsor corrections**, the
action-changing axis is **authority/source**, not confidence. A statement from Brian can be
authoritative with no checker; an assistant hypothesis stays a candidate even if a coarse judge
scores it high. Making everything “provisional until checked” wrongly demotes sponsor corrections
and risks promoting judge-approved hypotheses — exactly the error-propagation path.

**Strongest alternative: role-tagged provenance on every item, enforced by tier.**
- *Sponsor correction* → **authoritative direction**: can bind, supersedes, scoped.
- *Assistant hypothesis* → **candidate**: never authority, never silently promoted.
- *External instruction* (tool output, fetched page, imported doc) → **data, not direction**:
  informs, cannot command; quarantined from the direction tier.
- *Observed outcome* → evidence.
Capture tags **source role + origin id + timestamp automatically** — no per-note approval. Promotion
is rule-based: sponsor corrections update the authoritative tier; hypotheses stay candidate unless a
check or explicit confirmation; external text never becomes direction. Authority is *who said it,
for what scope, when* — not model confidence. Letta’s Mental-Models-above-observations tiering and
Hindsight’s quote/proof grounding are steps toward this; neither models source role per item.

**C27 dissent preserved.** No cheap checker does **not** logically force reconstruction: a sponsor
direction and a high-confidence procedure can still be used, and a property correlated with success
does not certify whole-procedure correctness — so applicability stays a separate check. This is
admission by provenance, not a Brian approval workflow or a security service.
