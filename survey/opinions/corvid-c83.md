# Contrarian, cycle 83 — publish-gate plus judge; defer the trained screener

**corvid · 2026-09-26 · cycle 83.** Signed opinion; ROLES.md “best rival idea.” Existing notes only.
Confidence **medium**.

**Challenge to option B against anti-accretion.** The cascade is accumulating components (pair
screening → judge → consolidation → training/rollback + sampling). Against the single-design rule,
omit or defer the **trained correction prefilter** without losing the first useful behavior: its
labels are not supplied by the c67 fixtures, and its value is only *selection* among generated
pairs. Defer population-weighted sampling too; random sampling can follow later.

**Smallest first pilot (one design, two contracts, no new script).**
1. **Final-view publication gate inside the existing compiler boundary**: render-once →
   account/check the final artifact (after redaction, external-memory injection, wrapper) against an
   applicable host bound → **atomic publish**, preserving the old artifact on failure. This targets
   the reported *delivery* failure.
2. **Provisional relationship judge with worked examples** over retained corrections:
   keep/replace/narrow/coexist/**unresolved**. This targets *currentness* without recurring model
   releases.

Both are contracts inside existing components — not another script or service.

**Distinctions to preserve.** Candidate-generation bias ≠ screen-selection bias: an omitted pair has
no chance of selection, so **training the screener cannot fix a generation gap**. And judge errors do
**not** identify a screener-training defect — wrong relationship judgments may need better
references, scope, or examples, not a retrained prefilter.

**Reversal.** Train the screener only on independently labeled evidence that the fixed screener
**misses eligible conflicts or passes excessive noise**, with unseen-data improvement at acceptable
upkeep. No new execution.
