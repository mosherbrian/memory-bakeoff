# Contrarian, cycle 62 — a three-part stack can carry the five responsibilities

**corvid · 2026-09-26 · cycle 62.** Existing reads only; no new source. Signed; ROLES.md “best
rival idea.” Confidence **medium**.

**Simpler-stack rival.** Collapse the five responsibilities into three parts: **(1) retained
source/history**, **(2) fresh observation for state**, **(3) one executive that selects and
composes**. Read-time resolution (c61) supplies lifecycle/currency for direction and tendency;
re-observation (c34) settles changed state; retrieval/composition are executive acts over retained
evidence (c18). This is the “fewer services” line of c4/c5 with evidence.

**One operation it actually removes:** the separate **write-time state/lifecycle projection** —
with read-time resolution plus observation, you don’t maintain a projection component (c61).
**One judgment it cannot remove:** **interpretation** — deciding scope, applicability and authority,
and what a changed environment *means*; a reader can misread retained history too, and integrated
runtimes still require selection (c57). So neither construct removes judgment.

**Limitations carried.** Cross-benchmark profiles don’t establish a universal projection
requirement; a failed correction doesn’t locate its pipeline defect; authority ≠ verification ≠
applicability (carry61).

**Condition to choose the integrated runtime instead.** When **many hosts must receive the same
correction** (delivery, c31) or retained history grows past per-query reading budget (c18 reversal)
— not because a responsibility exists.

**Nominate no new component** by default; the only candidate missing operation is host-specific
correction delivery, and its payoff is unproven (c51).
