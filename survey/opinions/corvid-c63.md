# Contrarian, cycle 63 — the composer is the lever, not the store

**corvid · 2026-09-26 · cycle 63.** Signed opinion; ROLES.md “best rival idea.” Source: Lost in the
Middle, Liu et al., arXiv:2307.03172v3 (20 Nov 2023), methods/controls. `[read]` Confidence
**medium**.

**Strongest rival.** The controlled placement/length evidence strengthens a **deliberate
composition step** more than a small core or selective retrieval alone. Retrieval decides *what* is
in context; **composition decides how much and where** — and the paper shows performance degrades
when decisive evidence is **buried in the middle** or the supplied context grows. So the load-bearing
responsibility is **one context composer** (L5): placement plus budget, not merely accountability.
A small always-present core and selective retrieval are useful mainly because they **shorten** the
composed context and keep the decisive item near an edge.

**One action.** For preference/procedure decisions, **shrink the composed context and place the
decisive direction/method at an edge** rather than mixing it among many retrieved items or replaying
the whole history. **Reversal.** If the host shows no positional sensitivity (unmeasured here), or
the decisive item must stay with its supporting evidence, prioritise **length reduction and retrieval
precision** over edge placement.

**Limits.** v3 tested older models; do **not** infer a universal first/last recipe or a current
failure rate. Carry62: a responsibility alone implies no service; LongMemEval did not establish a
matched victory over write-time supersession.
