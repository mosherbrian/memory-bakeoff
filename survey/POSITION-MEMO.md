# Agent memory: keep what was learned, recheck what changed

> State tells the agent what to do now. Memory tells it what it has learned. History lets it reconstruct what happened. Artifacts establish what is true. Executive reasoning decides what it means.
>
> Don't delete the past just because you stop putting it in every prompt.

— **Brian**, [principles](inputs/BRIAN-PRINCIPLES.md).

**Tern · cycle37 ·26 September2026.** Brian's costs are **re-learning procedures first, repeating preferences second**. My recommendation is a small, agent-maintained body of reusable guidance, one source for explicit directions, and recoverable supporting history. **Medium confidence; no product winner established.** Brian should not become its librarian. Start with existing facilities; agents own capture, revision and host refresh. The complete delivery path remains to be established.

**Three ranked bets**

1. **Save the reusable method; reconstruct present conditions.** Keep ordering, gotchas, applicability and useful failed alternatives. Fix a recurring mechanical problem in tooling or defaults when possible; use textual skills for judgment and helpers for stable steps. Observe consequential changing state when a cheap, safe check can settle it; past success is not current success. Revise on meaningful changes, without universal per-run recertification. Recovery notes can help one job; agents can also distill lessons and examples for later tasks. Allow useful provisional methods; keep their uncertainty visible. Reverse this bet when reconstruction costs less than upkeep and misapplication. [Procedure evidence](reading/tern-c2-outcome-evidence.md), [imperfect outcomes](panel-response-c27.md).
2. **Share durable learning by scope; hand off the current task.** Keep explicit directions in one authoritative source with host views, separate from inferred tendencies. Preserve intent and supersession: a quoted user sentence is not automatically a standing instruction. Share corrections and learned procedures across compatible hosts; pass live objectives, state and decisive evidence explicitly. Shared files already qualify—service access does not guarantee that a correction reaches an existing prompt or gets used. Repeated copies of one source are not independent corroboration. Preserve disagreeing accounts; resolve their scope and current relevance without silently erasing either. Reverse promotion or sharing when it spreads mis-scoped guidance faster than correction. [Preference evidence](reading/tern-c3-preference-evidence.md), [sharing judgment](panel-response-c29.md).
3. **Keep the past recoverable; select what guides the next action.** Preserve failures and superseded directions without continually recommending them. Retention, discovery and applicability are separate. Withdraw invalid current guidance while keeping a usable recovery path; low success after retrieval alone does not establish invalidity. Resolve short histories while reading; maintain a recoverable current view when repeated resolution becomes costly. [Retirement](panel-response-c26.md), [recovery](reading/tern-c18-reading-and-host-path.md).

**What to buy:** compare missing operations, not “native versus integrated.” ReMe offers file-compatible capture/consolidation; Hindsight offers derived-observation machinery; Letta offers an integrated runtime. Compare the current operations and version, not a historical paper pipeline. Each has costs and unverified behavior on Brian's hosts. Agents can also maintain ordinary files. Choose automation that removes actual work; price acquisition, retries, evaluation, upkeep and user attention as well as retrieval. Small-model self-curation can help without proving total affordability. [Alternatives](ALTERNATIVES.md), [roster](ROSTER.md).

**The roadmap still holds as responsibilities, not necessarily five services** (medium confidence):

| Layer | Keep this responsibility |
|---|---|
| Lossless history | Recover source events, failures and corrections after prompt exclusion. |
| State/lifecycle projection | Separate next-action state from learned guidance; preserve scope and revision. |
| Semantic/causal retrieval | Find evidence and counterexamples; retrieved rationale is not causal proof. |
| Bounded working memory | Supply a useful view without destroying unknown future relevance. |
| One context composer | Make precedence and budgeting accountable; leave interpretation to the executive. |

Artifacts and executive reasoning cross every layer. SKILL.state, Perseus and pi-lcm remain role candidates, not settled components. [Role/layer map](FIELD-MAP.md#five-roles-mapped-onto-the-five-roadmap-layers).

**Dissents:** accept Corvid’s agent-owned insight extraction, but authoring judgments move to the model rather than disappear. Reject Kiln’s universal firewall against carrying recovery notes forward: selective distillation is supported, categorical exclusion is not. Cairn’s transfer controls strengthen later-task learning while leaving acquisition economics open. [Current response](panel-response-c37.md), [earlier positions](snapshots/POSITION-MEMO-c28.md).

**Overrated:** treating stored, retrieved or versioned guidance as demonstrated useful learning. **Open frontier:** durable benefit under changing conditions with less user effort. Existing benchmarks and our narrow local results do not yet choose the upkeep/delivery winner.
