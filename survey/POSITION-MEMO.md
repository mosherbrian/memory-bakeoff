# Agent memory: keep what was learned, recheck what changed

> State tells the agent what to do now. Memory tells it what it has learned. History lets it reconstruct what happened. Artifacts establish what is true. Executive reasoning decides what it means.
>
> Don't delete the past just because you stop putting it in every prompt.

— **Brian**, [principles](inputs/BRIAN-PRINCIPLES.md).

**Tern · cycle52 ·26 September2026.** Brian's costs are **re-learning procedures first, repeating preferences second**. My recommendation is a small, agent-maintained body of reusable guidance, one source for explicit directions, and recoverable supporting history. **Medium confidence; no product winner established.** Brian should not become its librarian. Start with existing facilities; agents draft, revise and refresh guidance. The complete delivery path remains to be established.

**Three ranked bets**

1. **Retain the useful method in the form that fits.** Keep the smallest useful explanation, example, parameterized routine or generated helper—not a required pipeline for every recurrence. Preserve ordering, conditions, alternatives and source evidence. Helpers may observe and branch; prose can guide checks and interpretation. Prefer a worthwhile tool/default fix to repeated mechanical instructions. Avoid duplicate guidance that merely restates it; retain useful rationale and exceptions. Observe consequential changing state when a cheap, safe check can settle it; past success is not current success. Before revising, distinguish a bad rule, bad application and changed conditions; avoid universal recertification. Agents can draft reusable artifacts, while recovery notes may help only the current job. Choose author and executor capability for the task; reliable local upkeep is not established. Keep uncertainty visible. Reverse this bet when reconstruction costs less than upkeep and misapplication. [Procedure evidence](reading/tern-c2-outcome-evidence.md), [current synthesis](panel-response-c45.md).
2. **Share durable learning by scope; hand off the current task.** Keep explicit directions in one authoritative source with host views, separate from inferred tendencies. Agents may turn useful examples into editable inferred rules without policy training; keep scope and attribution revisable, especially for hypothetical or third-person material. Preserve intent and supersession: a quoted user sentence is not automatically a standing instruction. Judge preference memory by useful responses and action outcomes under actual constraints, not recall or expected-call selection alone; no extra check is universally required. Put deterministic project choices in suitable defaults when worthwhile; use actual host load paths for scoped guidance. Share corrections across compatible hosts; pass live objectives, state and decisive evidence explicitly. Shared files already qualify—service access does not guarantee that a correction reaches an existing prompt or gets used. Repeated copies of one source are not independent corroboration. Preserve disagreeing accounts; resolve their scope and current relevance without silently erasing either. Reverse promotion or sharing when it spreads mis-scoped guidance faster than correction. [Preference evidence](PREFERENCE-EVIDENCE.md), [sharing judgment](panel-response-c29.md).
3. **Keep the past recoverable; select what guides the next action.** Preserve failures and superseded directions without continually recommending them. Retention, discovery and applicability are separate; selective loading must repay its added judgment and cost. Withdraw invalid current guidance while keeping a usable recovery path; low success after retrieval alone does not establish invalidity. Resolve short histories while reading; maintain a recoverable current view when repeated resolution becomes costly. [Retirement](panel-response-c26.md), [recovery](reading/tern-c18-reading-and-host-path.md).

**What to buy:** compare missing operations, not “native versus integrated.” ReMe offers file-compatible capture/consolidation; Hindsight offers derived-observation machinery; Letta offers an integrated runtime. Compare the current operations and version, not a historical paper pipeline. Each has costs and unverified behavior on Brian's hosts. Agents can also maintain ordinary files. Choose automation that removes actual work; price acquisition, retries, evaluation, upkeep and user attention as well as retrieval. Small-model self-curation can help without proving total affordability. [Alternatives](ALTERNATIVES.md), [roster](ROSTER.md).
