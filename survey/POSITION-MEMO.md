# Agent memory: the architecture I would keep, and what I would defer

**Tern · cycle 1, v0.1 · 26 September 2026 · Explore.** For Brian's Claude Code, Pi, local models, and admin/rollout work. Recommendations, not deployed changes. [Required roadmap](inputs/PHASE2_ROADMAP.md) and [reconciliation](inputs/PHASE2_ROADMAP_RECONCILIATION.md) restore the survey's purpose: **B field refresh, C roster refresh, F architecture synthesis**.

**Position:** the five-layer architecture still holds as a division of responsibilities, **medium confidence**. It does not yet justify five services or the original product choices. My earlier notes-first framing underplayed the history underneath the notes. Keep retained source history recoverable; derive small, revisable views for work.

| Roadmap layer | Does it still hold? My bet for Brian | Confidence / what would change it |
|---|---|---|
| **1. Lossless canonical history** | **Yes, as the foundation.** Preserve authorized raw events, source links, and branch identity; a summary must not replace the evidence it summarizes. Apply explicit retention/deletion choices rather than silently losing history. pi-lcm remains a candidate, not a selected winner. | **Medium** on architecture; product fit open. Change if native history already supplies recovery and replay adequately, making another substrate redundant. |
| **2. State/lifecycle projection** | **Yes, with stronger priority.** Distinguish current from historical belief, corrected from superseded, concurrent scopes/configurations, and failed from successful procedures. Derive this view from sources; record event validity separately from when we learned it where needed. Recheck volatile operational state. | **Medium.** Change the implementation if manual/agent update burden or false supersession outweighs fewer stale actions. |
| **3. Semantic/causal retrieval** | **Yes to source-backed deep recall; defer a dedicated causal engine.** Search history as well as current views. Start with lexical search, add semantic/routing mechanisms for named misses. Finding a rationale or dependency is not proving causation. | **Medium** on need; **low** on best mechanism. Change if native full context is cheaper and adequate, or representative paraphrase/history misses justify richer retrieval. |
| **4. Bounded working-memory synthesis** | **Yes, as a disposable working view.** Use scoped preferences, current-task handoff, and relevant procedures. Keep links back to history. Do not force an aggressively tiny window or require the participant to maintain a complex state machine. | **Medium.** Change if compaction loses constraints or recovery loops erase its context savings. |
| **5. One context composer** | **Yes as one accountable composition policy**, ideally within the existing host: instructions, recent context, working view, and targeted recall. Multiple providers are fine; competing owners of truncation and precedence are not a good default. | **Medium** as design judgment; benefit unproven. Change if a simpler native policy meets the same needs with less integration work. |

These judgments use the [roadmap and seven-failure analysis](FIELD-MAP.md#roadmap-layers-and-the-seven-failure-lens), not a product leaderboard. **Important negative:** the [Gen45 Pi pilot](inputs/PI_STATE_CONTROL_GEN45_LIVE_PILOT.md) passed **7/12** versus stock Pi's **12/12**, with more cumulative context. Its control tools were largely unused; it tests that composed-window implementation, not the full architecture. **High confidence locally; limited transfer.**

**Ranked practical bets:** (1) preserve/reuse history and make the current handoff explicit; (2) improve correction, scope, and procedure handling where actual mistakes occur; (3) buy retrieval or synthesis complexity only for a demonstrated gap. Small source-linked notes remain useful, but curation versus automatic capture is unresolved. R68 supports a narrow saved-preference benefit; R53 shows retrieval can add no marginal outcome value. **Medium confidence in usefulness, low–medium in ordering.** [R68](inputs/R68-acceptance.json), [R53](inputs/R53-acceptance.json).

**Overrated:** a universal memory winner, or per-request token reduction as proof of total savings. **Frontier:** rebuilding trustworthy scoped state while recovering old evidence and composing affordable context. The [reading shortlist](ROSTER.md) refreshes mechanisms without restarting a contestant queue.

**Dissents:** [Corvid](opinions/corvid-c1-roadmap.md) champions history plus on-demand retrieval, with maintained state added only when needed (**medium**). **Accept** this rival: the five responsibilities do not prove five persistent components; reconstruction at read time remains viable. His automatic-capture versus selective-notes disagreement stays **open**. [Kiln](opinions/kiln-c1-roadmap.md) watches pi-lcm/OM and routed retrieval (**low–medium**). **Accept** the mechanism shortlist; **reject** mandatory Phase-D admission for exploratory reading and the implication that a baseline was newly deployed. No experiment follows automatically. [Responses](panel-response-c1.md).
