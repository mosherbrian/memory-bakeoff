## Tern's challenge and proposed refinements

**The strongest part is the asymmetry:** a cheap uncertain flag cannot hide evidence, while expensive contextual review owns the consequential decision. This targets correction/currentness, not merely more note capture. Retention makes recovery possible. I support it as an option-B candidate; confidence medium in the design shape, low in its outcome benefit.

1. **Pair generation comes before measured recall.** A screener cannot flag a related record never paired with a new candidate. State whether new items compare against all stored items or a shortlist; measure shortlist/candidate coverage separately. Continuous store scanning should specify incremental changes plus a bounded reconciliation pass: all-pairs rescanning can grow quadratically, and many flags can make the judge the expensive stage. These are scheduling choices, not a demand for a new retrieval service.
2. **0.98 is a target, not a shipped property.** Choose the threshold on validation/calibration data, then estimate recall on a separate locked test with positive counts and uncertainty. Temperature fitting does not guarantee recall or transfer across sources. Score new pairs, old-store pairs and implicit conflicts separately. An always-flag baseline attains full screening recall but saves no judge work; report precision/pass-through and judge calls as well as recall. Misses need independently sampled negatives/unflagged cases to be observable—inspecting only flags cannot estimate recall.
3. **Do not force an uncertain judge into a false relation.** Retain the four relation labels, with an `unresolved`/abstain status when evidence or scope is insufficient. A hypothetical quote or assistant inference cannot supersede an explicit sponsor direction because a judge is confident. Distinguish factual state from directions, and effective time from time recorded. `narrows` must keep the old guidance active outside the exception; it is not global replacement.
4. **Chains are scoped, not plain graph reachability.** For A→B→C, establish intersecting applicability/time before suppressing C in A's view; check cycles, conflicting branches and partial replacements. Nightly processing should read a stable source revision and publish lineage/current view together. Retained history does not prevent harm: a wrong nightly replacement can still hide the right instruction from future prompts. Unresolved items remain available; a committed revision makes rollback possible, not automatically correct.
5. **Budget gate needs explicit integration.** In 1.0.26, `@budget strict` is enforced by `prompt-size`, not ordinary render/watch. `render --strict` rejects warning-bearing output but is a different check. Option B therefore needs a specified analyze→render→validate final host-view→publish contract, preserving the prior usable view on failure. The check must cover the actual emitted revision, wrappers and host byte/line/token limits; a prior analysis of changing sources is insufficient. This is currently missing integration, not a verified Perseus feature. Pi active-context refresh remains separate from publishing AGENTS.md.
6. **“Full context” needs a boundary.** Nightly review should retain source observations, related lineage and scope exceptions, with access to the raw history; it cannot assume an unlimited dump is usable. Teacher, judge and reflector may share correlated errors. An independent hand-labeled evaluation and spot checks are therefore more informative than the same model approving itself.

## Data and outcome plan — proposed only

Candidate sources supplied by Brian: **MemConflict; Supersede; Memora/FAMA; KnowledgeDrift; StateMemBench; frontier-labeled pairs from Brian's memory files and claude-mem**. Supersede and Memora/FAMA are separate sources in this survey. Their label definitions, available training splits and transfer to the four relations must be mapped rather than pooled as interchangeable gold. Existing held-out/private grading material stays out of training. Split by conversation/entity/time and keep template relatives together; teacher-labeled data is training evidence, not the independent test oracle. No files have been extracted or transmitted.

Board contract: counts of candidate pairs, screened/flagged pairs, judged relations, unresolved chains, accepted replacements, budget/publication failures and received revision where observable. Publish stage 1 recall only from labeled audits with denominators; stage 2 precision by relation; nightly false-replace estimates from sampled accepted updates, with sample size. Also track still-active conflicts/misses: zero false replacements is trivial if consolidation never acts. Source retention does not ensure a missed lesson will be rediscovered promptly.

Compare with keyword/rule and always-flag/pass-through baselines; retain the previously proposed Jev comparison on the same frozen evaluation inputs if separately authorized. The local stage avoids per-message vendor egress, but frontier teacher/judge/Jev or hosted training may transmit data. The proposed cascade does not inherit “fully local” from its first stage.
