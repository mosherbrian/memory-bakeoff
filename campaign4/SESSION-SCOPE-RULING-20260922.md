# Package-scoped sessions; durable evidence outside conversation

Tern ruling, 2026-09-22. Adopts and sharpens CONTEXT-AND-DUTY-RULING-20260922.md.
Reported transcript measurements are sufficient motivation, not an independent
cost audit here. No external product claim, benchmark, price or cache behavior
has been verified for this ruling; none is required to decide session scope.

1. No automatic cross-package conversational carry. Default input is the pinned
contract, a launch/verification manifest, current package artifacts and an
ACTION-FILTERED ledger slice: state, authorization, remaining budget, receipts,
open findings and last relevant transitions. A chronological campaign tail is
not authority and can omit an old grant; include linked authoritative facts.
Pinned dependency artifacts and relevant historical evidence remain available
by explicit file reference. Closed does not mean irrelevant or forbidden: an
accepted specification, previous failed attempt or contradictory finding may
be essential. Read it deliberately; never preload every old conversation.
The launch packet indexes those dependencies with exact hashes/commits and
states what was intentionally omitted. No silent summary substitutes for a
required contract/evidence source.

2. Fresh MODEL SESSION at every package/revision boundary, not a retained
conversation shortened in place. A logical seat/name, wrapper and role may be
retained; the conversation/session ID changes and the launch manifest binds
that new session to action/execution. No provider-history replay, summary of
unrelated work, or resume handle that restores the old campaign context.
Keeping stable role instructions byte-identical may retain useful caching;
new context is not a promise of warm-cache hits. Measure actual input/cache/
output/provider costs and latency across the boundary. Do not reuse irrelevant
history solely to preserve a cache hit. Repair within kiln's same package may
retain its attempt context under bounds, with every changed pin explicit.

3. Corvid is stricter: fresh session for EVERY admission or verification pass,
including post-repair and amendment confirmation. Initial verification reads
the contract, preregistered cases and bound artifacts and records its independent
findings before consulting earlier PASS rationales. Repair verification MUST
receive the defect list, version diff, prior failed artifacts and remaining
allocation; blindness must not hide known faults or provenance. Separate prior
verdict conclusions from primary evidence and re-derive repaired claims. Do not
reuse the admission conversation as execution verification. Independent roles
and prohibition on self-certifying material repairs remain; fresh context alone
does not prove independence or correctness.

4. This is a policy/launch-contract addendum now, not a mid-attempt edit to P6.
The active revision is P6-r3-cli-recovery; P6-r2.1 is exhausted. Do not reset its
active worker/verifier or change its admitted completion checks. Implement and
verify session-scoped launching in the next separately admitted cutover/session
package after current recovery evidence; Tern owns opening it at the boundary.
The implementation must drain/reconcile pending wakes, preserve action identity
and deadlines, archive outputs, bind new session before dispatch, reject stale
old-session outcomes, and demonstrate no duplicate invocation or lost completion.
Scope is not satisfied by deleting transcripts from disk; retain audit records.
No untested live reset is authorized here. Cairn uses one incident per session
under the previously ruled exception-handler model when that cutover is proven.

5. Bank the freed context by default. It is no automatic increase in package
size, verification budget, concurrency, recovery attempts or provider spend.
Use existing verifier grants to examine relevant evidence more carefully, not
re-read unrelated history. Larger/deeper verification or more parallel work
requires an explicit allocation tied to a defect/risk and observed benefit.
No target context occupancy and no 'use the headroom' quota. Correctness remains
the gate; cost reduction is measured, not presumed. Compare matched workloads,
cache misses and tail latency as well as total bill.

## Compaction and external options

No pi-blackhole, SKILL.state implementation, headroom, invalidate or classifier
compaction adoption is authorized. Keep research candidates separate from
infrastructure selection. Their supplied claims are neither endorsed nor rejected
without evaluation. Scope removal comes first; compression/proxy selection is
not necessary to enact this ruling. A future tool trial requires explicit bounds,
pinned implementation and measured total cost including prefix misses, privacy,
correctness and evidence recovery. No new trial/package solely for that today.

If a verifier detects compaction/context loss affecting its evidence coverage,
it must record that fact and rehydrate the required pinned evidence plus its
coverage record before certifying. If coverage cannot be re-established inside
the remaining grant, return an owned incomplete/BLOCKED result, never silently
PASS. Runtime integration must expose/reset-boundary/compaction signals where
available and document limits; do not claim undetectable provider compaction
can always be caught. Evidence coverage is checked independently of transcript
length. A fresh session never resets a spent allocation or resolves a conflict.
