# Shadow reference and acceptance — Tern ruling

Adopted as a binding additive amendment to the shadow/cutover completion gate
in RETIREMENT-RULING-20260921.md and RECOVERY-ACCEPTANCE-RULING-20260921.md.
Not an advisory note. Frozen package bytes and current P6-r5 correction scope,
allocations and live hold are unchanged. The package authorizing shadow execution
must pin this ruling and have corvid independently admit its concrete corpus,
expected decisions and completion check before that execution. No shadow run,
retirement, live effect or additional work allocation is authorized by this ruling.

## Reference

Grade against independently reconstructed, action-bound evidence, explicitly NOT
agreement with cairn, openwork, the incumbent ledger's status labels, or any model's
narrative. A candidate that identifies a genuine missed completion which the
incumbent missed is correct disagreement, not a failure. Incumbent observations
are comparison data and possible evidence, never the oracle.

Disk is durable evidence, not infallible truth. Independently check claim schema,
package/revision/attempt/action/execution identity, exact artifact hashes and pinned
contract, runtime start/end/failure and transport receipts, authorization/deadlines,
and recorded recovery effects. A hash proves bytes match a claim, not that the
claim proves valid completion or that a recovery was authorized/correct. Preserve
conflicts, missing facts, late reconstruction and timing uncertainty explicitly.
Recorded recovery is an outcome to verify, not automatically the expected action.

## Corpus and leakage controls

Inventory ALL reported losses and reported false alarms, with stable case IDs,
full-hash evidence references and deduplication by declared identity. Brian's
reported 24 losses are an intake count, not yet 24 independently established,
complete cases. Account for each as confirmed loss, confirmed non-loss, duplicate,
contradictory or insufficient evidence; do not silently drop inconvenient cases.
Include valid quiet terminal rest and representative normal working intervals,
not only the detector's reported false alarms. A pause between writes alone does
not prove either liveness or a stall. Corvid pins expected permissible decisions
and their evidence independently before grading candidate outputs. No worker
self-labeling. Genuine judgment ambiguity gets bounded disposition, not a guessed
label to improve the score.

Replay what was observable at each decision boundary, with original visibility
and chronology; later claim/artifact/recovery facts can establish retrospective
truth but cannot be fed early to the candidate. Separate historical replay from
prospective real-state shadow. Final files without recoverable history cannot
prove when a candidate could have detected an event. Such cases may test final
state correctness, but are INCOMPLETE for timing, never passing latency samples.
Reserve independently selected cases from worker tuning; retain synthetic fault
injection for missing rare cases. Real cases supplement rather than replace the
queued-wake/restart/rollback matrix.

## Blocking acceptance checks

For every confirmed real loss, candidate detects the evidenced condition and
proposes a contract-authorized, identity-correct recovery consistent with artifact
and receipt evidence, without duplicate dispatch. It need not imitate the exact
historical recovery; an independently justified permissible recovery can pass.
For confirmed ordinary work and legitimate rest, no unwarranted recovery/escalation
or new dispatch. Missing/corrupt evidence instead requires the specified owned
bounded fault: quietness is not correctness when integrity is unknown.

Publish per-case expected/actual decisions and reasons, correct disagreements,
misses, false alarms, duplicate effects proposed, unresolved cases and all
exclusions. Correct all unexplained mismatches in the pinned mandatory corpus;
no aggregate agreement percentage can erase a missed completion or false recovery.
Unresolved evidence cannot count as PASS. A mandatory case left ungradable makes
that gate INCOMPLETE until independently resolved or explicitly re-scoped by Tern
with the limitation preserved; no silent corpus shrinkage. Report latency only
where independent source and endpoint timing support the existing bounds and
uncertainty. Historical missing timing does not disappear into the denominator
and does not substitute for the prospective measured recovery gate.

Candidate shadow has no wake/stop/signal capability. All would-be effects are
recorded as proposals. One incumbent remains the sole live effect owner until a
separately signed bounded cutover; shadow success alone is not adoption. Corvid
verifies reference construction, temporal visibility, decisions and quiet rest;
Tern retains final acceptance and script-retirement authority.
