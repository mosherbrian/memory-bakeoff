# R63 diagnosis
R62 repaired the three literal R61 label mechanisms but narrowed the release: (a) it stopped reading prose, so explicit guessing/contradiction in prose could reach primary unattended; (b) its log regex extracted substrings, so a malformed suffix still counted, "25..0" crashed float(), and a malformed ctx silently became no-run. Author and reviewer tests checked the candidate's own table, not the release boundary. Remedy here: strict full-line trace grammar (actual fixture format), strict report grammar with exact decimals, a mechanical candidate grade, and a separate deterministic gate that makes every final disposition depend on a hash-bound independent adjudication receipt. No prose classifier; semantic judgement is the reviewer's, recorded in the receipt.
